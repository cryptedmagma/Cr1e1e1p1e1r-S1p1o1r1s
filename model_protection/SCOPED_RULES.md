# Creeper Spores — scoped modeling protections and study rules

## Decision semantics
- Ignore excluded/CUT entries **completely** when reasoning, checking contradictions, or deciding edits; their positions are retained only as empty slots to keep question numbering reversible.
- Do not use response order or section position as priority. Previous and later reports coexist; a new answer cannot silently repeal an earlier safeguard.
- A **Yes** applies only to the specific question, component, mechanism and condition. A **No** declines that proposition; it is **not** a grant of editing permission.
- Contradiction requires directly opposite non-excluded instructions for exactly the same feature and conditions. Context differences are not conflicts.
- The 15 candidate questions per feature protect comparison activity where affirmed; they do not choose a winner, approve combining versions or authorize release.
- A request to study design, texture, pivots or animation does not authorize changing the shipped addon. Copy the baseline, keep recoverable source, and obtain explicit approval for a proposed change to a protected component.

## Concept targets / existing safeguards / permitted study

### Swamp (first)
Target: irregular broad lily-pad crown with front notch, raised white lotus and golden center, moss hanging from the pad rim, muddy head, reeds and cattails near feet. The new report affirms specific safeguards for 89 named feature areas and candidate comparisons for those supported features. The excluded 11 opening feature positions do not yield a decision. From the earlier clean study, all 100 named features had affirmative scoped safeguards under their earlier conditions. Study pad lobes, rim, lotus petals, visibility and moss drape on a duplicate; preserve the current installed model until an exact edit is approved. No whole lower-body redesign.

### Crystal (second)
Target: asymmetrical connected amethyst clusters around the head and feet, with visible eyes/mouth and readable negative spaces. The new report answers Yes to all 8,000 scoped questions. Earlier protection specifically covers full body silhouette, total height, head proportions/planes, eyes/mouth, head-neck connection, stalk and protected anatomy, while other crystal-size proposals remain unresolved. Study tip direction, base thickness, perspective, attachment and clipping **without replacing the protected core**.

### Jungle (third)
Target: wide tropical umbrella canopy, hanging foliage, one red flower and waist leaves. The new report answers Yes to all 8,000 scoped protection questions. Earlier reports had narrower study focus: front-leaf shadows, hue, contrast, gaps and face crowding. These different conditions coexist; the new protection answers do not themselves authorize a new canopy or flower.

### Lava (fourth)
Target: heavy dark basalt head, physically connected molten crown, continuous magma seams and glowing feet. The new report answers Yes to all 8,000 protection questions. Earlier report's safe change-specific study was limited to the torso/front-leg connection and rollback for worse geometry. Preserve previous scoped protections over drips, fissures, basalt, legs, feet, and material contrast; no crown/head replacement without approval.

### Thorn (fifth)
Target: forward-curving woody stalk, connected roots/vines, unevenly tapered ivory thorns and readable gaps. The new report contains 840 affirmative, four declined and 7,156 excluded responses; its first 11 features affirm pre-edit approval requirements, while the remaining first-question positions are excluded. Earlier study/protection explicitly safeguards most described parts, including portions not affirmed in this later report. Study lower stalk, growth directions, root connections, projection and animation clearance; keep unrelated geometry unchanged.

### Spore Queen (sixth)
Target: broad rounded peach-pink cap with scalloped edge and raised center, purple gills, pale stalk, broad violet draped skirt, cream toes, moss and ground mushrooms. The new report has many excluded and declined protection requests; **none implies permission** to redesign. Earlier protections specifically cover gown/folds/shoulders, foot mushrooms and toes, shading, spore relationships and other named details. Study cap, separate lobes, gills, facial shadow and stalk attachment on duplicates; retain the existing protected gown and lower growth unless approved.

## Modeling and verification acceptance
1. Follow Swamp → Crystal → Jungle → Lava → Thorn → Spore Queen, one localized proposal at a time, keeping old sources and candidate versions.
2. Mark the exact component, view, expected visual difference, file/bone, animation condition and authorization before building an alternative.
3. Compare reference silhouettes at matched scale and camera (front, sides, rear, top, 3/4); inspect textured and gray views separately.
4. Reject changes making shapes less recognizable, producing flat canopy roofs, candle-like spikes, detached molten flows, missing petal roots, hidden faces, clipping, floating parts or degraded game-distance readability.
5. Verify pivot/bone names, geometry/UV/JSON, all alternate/charged textures, pack identifiers and file integrity; unrelated assets must remain byte-identical.
6. Build a new addon only after explicit authorized merge; software renders are not Minecraft runtime proof. Record in-game tests separately.
