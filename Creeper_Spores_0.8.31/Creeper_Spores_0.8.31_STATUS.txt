09 CARRIERS — reported defects
  - Reported: infected mobs did not release spores on death, and carrier infection was lost after
    save/reload. Root causes found in source:
      a) Mob carriers expired 2400 ticks of ABSOLUTE world time after pickup. Unloaded chunks,
         time away and a closed world all consumed that window, so carriers were wiped on return
         and had nothing to release when they died.
      b) Death deposits accepted only air cells, so dying in grass, flowers or snow dropped an
         item instead of planting a pod.
      c) Player carriers lived only in memory, timed against a tick counter that restarts at 0 on
         every world load, so any reload lost them. Players also never released a spore on death.
  - Audit additions: spectators never become carriers; failed death releases are logged
    (mob_spore_death_no_valid_cell, player_spore_death_no_valid_cell); new read-only command
    /scriptevent cs:carrier shows your own carrier state and nearby infected mobs (strain, blocks
    walked, carried time, drip state).
  - Fixes: mob and player carrier windows now count saved ACTIVE ticks (the 2400-tick length is
    unchanged). Older saved mob carriers keep their old window, then switch. Death deposits may
    displace soft ground cover; the item fallback remains for blocked ground. Player carrier state
    is saved on the player and restored on load, and dying while carrying releases the strain
    nearby (still refused in quarantine zones).
  - Approved thresholds are unchanged and asserted: 15-block one-time 40% drip that leaves
    infection active, 20-block final deposit after 40 carried ticks, cleanse after a successful
    final deposit. SOURCE-CHANGED, OFFLINE-PASS. UNVERIFIED in-game; this needs a save/quit/reopen
    test on a disposable world.
