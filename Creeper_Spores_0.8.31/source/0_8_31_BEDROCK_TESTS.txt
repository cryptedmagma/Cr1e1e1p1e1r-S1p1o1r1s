Forced explosion test
1. Import the 0.8.31 .mcaddon with Minecraft closed. Create a new Creative disposable world, activate both Creeper Spores packs, and enable cheats.
2. To compare all strains quickly, run `/scriptevent cs:test_spores` in the disposable world. The fixture searches supported surfaces at the player and up to 32 blocks below. Wait at least 2 seconds for block ticks, then run `/scriptevent cs:inspect_spores`; it reports nearby live pod counts by strain and stages 0–7. Repeat after 30–60 seconds to confirm the stage counts increase. For the blast test, run `/scriptevent cs:test_blast`, move away, then use the inspector to confirm the raised seed's strain and age advance. Use `/scriptevent cs:diag` for rejection/error counters.
3. Put a supported surface (grass/dirt) in a clear, unquarantined area. Run `/summon minecraft:creeper ~ ~ ~`, then ignite the spawned vanilla creeper with flint and steel, or use `/summon minecraft:creeper ~ ~ ~ minecraft:become_charged` if supported by that game build.
4. For deterministic fuse testing, custom creepers expose `minecraft:start_exploding`: summon e.g. `/summon cookiedookie:moss_creeper ~ ~ ~`, then trigger `/event entity @e[type=cookiedookie:moss_creeper,c=1] minecraft:start_exploding`. Observe the 1.5-second fuse and the configured explosion. If no seed appears, run `/scriptevent cs:diag` and record whether the log reports `blast_missing_source_before`, `blast_missing_source_after`, `blast_event_unavailable`, `blast_no_valid_surface`, `blast_seed_write_failed`, or `blast_visible_seeded`.
5. Within about one second after the explosion, inspect nearby supported air cells. Expected: at least one larger, raised stage-3 spore after a normal eligible Creeper blast; a charged blast attempts two. Run `/scriptevent cs:inspect_spores` to confirm it advances to stage 4 and continues through the usual growth path. The all-strain fixture still places stage-0 spores. Verify there are no seeds inside a quarantine zone, through barriers, on unsupported cells, or over existing blocks.
6. Wait through multiple spore ticks and record whether each seed advances age, keeps its strain, survives save/reload, and eventually hatches under allowed conditions. Repeat with each strain and in water/substrate cases.
7. Test ordinary vanilla Creeper, all 23 custom Creepers and their themed status effects, charged custom Creeper, and non-Creeper explosions to verify source filtering. Verify extra probabilistic deposits, barriers, max-placement limits, deep-dark suppression, and no duplicate blast releases.
