CREEPER SPORES 0.8.31 — REBUILT CREEPER LOOKS
Minecraft Bedrock Edition only. Experimental. Built from the 0.8.8 source tree.

WHAT CHANGED IN 0.8.31
- Full redo (2026-11-23) of Swamp, Crystal, Jungle, Lava, Thorn and Spore Queen against their reference art and the targets in the owner's design-protection registry, keeping the protected parts: Thorn's head block and face and the Spore Queen's gown, cream toes and foot moss are copied pixel-for-pixel from the previous build (checked offline), and every creeper keeps the vanilla rig, the 8x8x8 head and the creeper face. Swamp: irregular lily-pad crown with a notch, white lotus, moss hanging from the rim, cattails and reeds. Crystal: connected amethyst clusters on the head and feet. Jungle: wide umbrella canopy, red hibiscus, vines, waist leaves, rooted feet. Lava: basalt head with a fused molten crown, drips, cheek streams and fissures. Thorn: braided wreath with long ivory thorns, vines and roots. Spore Queen: broad scalloped peach cap with purple gills and hooked tendrils, small pink mushrooms at her feet. The previous designs are kept in creeper_forge/designs_previous_0_8_31.py.
- Redrawn from new reference pictures (2026-11-22): Water Creeper (fully redone: green creeper wading in water with a lily pad hat and pink water lily), Lava, Thorn, Carved Wood and Slender Green.
- Walking trails (cosmetic, client-side particles): Water leaves lily pads on the ground (some with a flower) and has about four bubbles popping in and fading around it; Glowing leaves rising glow motes; Flower leaves pink and white petals; Lava leaves rising embers; Mushroom leaves tiny toadstools; Spore Queen leaves pink spores. Trails play only while the creeper is walking. The old Water look (blue body, seaweed, bubble blocks), the floating glow cubes (Glowing) and the mushrooms at the feet (Mushroom, Spore Queen) were removed from the models; the 2026-11-23 Spore Queen redo puts a few small pink mushrooms back on the ground at her feet (her spore trail stays).
- Carrier fixes (reported defects): infected mobs and players no longer lose their carried spore when the world is saved and reopened or a chunk unloads, because the carry window now counts saved active ticks instead of absolute world time. Dying while carrying now releases the spore, and mobs dying in grass, flowers or snow plant a pod instead of dropping an item. Approved thresholds are unchanged (15-block one-time 40% drip, 20 blocks plus 40 ticks for the final drop, 2400-tick window).
- Hybrid plants: Spore Bluebell, Spore Bracken and Spore Bogcap now each have four distinct growth stages. Previously all three shared one generic shape, and the cap piece rendered garbled because its UVs ran off the texture.
- Spore-Proof armor pieces now each block specific creeper-blast effects: Helmet blocks blindness, darkness and nausea; Chestplate blocks poison and hunger; Leggings block slowness; Boots block weakness.
- Carrier audit: spectators never pick up spores; failed death releases are logged; /scriptevent cs:carrier inspects your own carrier state and nearby infected mobs.
- Structure safeguards: ruins and structure nests never overwrite containers, crops or spore pods and never place inside quarantine zones (the structure-nest placer previously ignored quarantine).
- Release tooling: the 0.7.0 and 0.8.30 baselines are bundled read-only and hash-checked; BASELINE_LEDGER lists hashes of every earlier release; the builder no longer overwrites a different existing pack; audit_release_0_8_31.py verifies the finished release and writes SHA256SUMS; VISUAL_RECORDS gives a per-variant normal/charged/reference record.
- Vanilla villager trades were not added: doing so would replace Mojang's trade tables, which the decision register rules out.
- See 0_8_31_STATUS.txt for an evidence-by-evidence status against the project specification. Nothing in 0.8.31 has been run in Minecraft yet.
- All 23 Creeper variants have brand-new models and textures, rebuilt to match the supplied concept art (Moss, Cave, Crystal, Swamp, Desert, Snow, Jungle, Mushroom, Sculk, Nether, End, Water, Lava, Flower, Thorn, Phantom, Giant, Miniature, Glowing, Spore Queen, Grass Crown, Carved Wood totem, and Slender Green bamboo).
- Textures are now real pixel art: every cube face has its own region on the texture atlas and is painted individually (stone/moss mixes, lava and sculk veins, snow and slime drips, rope bands, the classic creeper face). The old models reused one 16x16 swatch for every face, which is what made them look blotchy.
- New details include moss mats with dangling strands and daisies, stalagmites and glowing ore, leaning amethyst shard clusters on the head and every foot, a lily pad with a water lily and cattails, a cactus hat and rope coils, snow caps with icicles, a palm-frond crown with hibiscus and vines, a spotted toadstool cap, sculk tendrils, crimson coral and ember particles, End horns, bubbles and kelp, flame crowns, flower wreaths, bone thorns, ragged phantom wings, a frilled cap and trailing robe for the Spore Queen, a skull face and tall-grass crown, a carved terracotta totem on stone feet, and a segmented bamboo stalk.
- Quality pass: every face now has baked soft lighting (bright top lip, darker base), ordered-dither shading between colour steps, and contact shadows under moss/slime patches.
- Lava, Nether, Sculk and End now carry continuous glowing seams instead of scattered dots, and the flowers on the Flower, Moss and Jungle creepers are crisp pixel-art blossoms. The Spore Queen's cap no longer covers her face, and she has a flared cape.
- Real glow: Cave, Crystal, Sculk, Nether, End, Lava, Glowing and Spore Queen use Bedrock's entity_emissive_alpha material, so their seams, ore, flames and particles light up in the dark. On those creepers the charged-skin arcs glow too. Untested in-game.
- Each variant still has its normal, charged, alternate, and alternate-charged skins. Charged skins carry crackling blue arcs.
- Rig bone names (body, head, leg0-leg3) are unchanged, so the walking legs, breathing animations, ignite flash, entity IDs, behaviors and spawn rules all work as before.
- The models were previewed in Blockbench 5.2 against each reference image. In-game appearance still needs a Minecraft check.
- The generator that builds these models lives in creeper_forge/ inside the source ZIP (python creeper_forge/run.py <variant> rebuilds one creeper).
- Offline tests now validate every creeper model: required rig bones, matching texture sizes for all four skins, per-face UVs inside the atlas with no shared regions, rotated cubes with pivots, and the signature crystal, phantom and jungle details.

CHANGES CARRIED FORWARD FROM 0.8.30
- The Phantom Creeper wing panels now angle outward and down in five mirrored pairs, giving the model a more ragged, draped silhouette like the supplied reference. Offline geometry checks validate the ten segments and their mirrored rotation data; the in-game silhouette remains unverified.
- The Crystal Creeper model now places a crystal detail on each of its four feet and angles its crown/body shards outward, matching the supplied concept more closely. Offline geometry checks cover the corrected right-foot positions and cube rotations; in-game visual parity still needs review.
- The Spore Controller has a fifth redstone mode, Auto Seed. While powered it queues one new microscopic spore beside a mature colony per cooldown, consumes one Spore Extract from an adjacent inventory, preserves the colony strain, and honors quarantine zones and containment barriers. The existing cooldown and bounded scan keep work finite.
- The four Spore-Proof armor pieces now have player attachables using Bedrock's armor geometry/render controller and separate 64x32 UV-aligned armor textures. Helmet, chest, leggings, and boots map to their matching armor layers. Offline checks validate the item binding and layer geometry; appearance while equipped still needs Minecraft testing.
- All 23 Creeper variants now tune player alert distances, fuse trigger/cancel distances, idle roaming speed, and pursuit speed. Target selectors, swell behavior, nearby sensors, and melee pursuit use consistent per-variant values; existing entity IDs, explosion powers/events, and special-movement groups are preserved. Offline schema assertions check the settings. Bedrock pathing and balance still need gameplay testing.
- The all-strain in-world test command now searches supported ground up to 32 blocks below the player before reporting a placement failure. It still only places on empty/water cells above supported blocks.
- Eligible vanilla and custom Creeper-family explosions now plant at least one larger, clearly visible stage-3 spore on the closest valid nearby surface. Charged explosions seed up to two. This skips the tiny stage-0 fleck and the still-small stage-2 model; ordinary microscopic deposits and manual stage-0 spores keep their original start stage, and blast seeds continue through the regular stage-4 mutation checkpoint.
- The seeds retain the existing strain/mutation rules and honor support checks, containment barriers, quarantine zones, loaded-area checks, and the per-blast placement cap.
- Fixed the 23-variant environmental-ability reader to floor fractional entity coordinates before querying surrounding blocks; which ensures mob positions consistently resolve to their actual block cells. Added a mocked regression check.
- Optional native nest generation now includes eight additional documented Bedrock biome tags: swamp, desert, savanna, mesa/badlands, mangrove swamp, cherry grove, mushroom island, and frozen biomes. This remains a separate, opt-in pack and applies to newly generated chunks only.
- Existing probabilistic microscopic deposits remain as additional spread. These can still add farther colonies after their configured germination delay.
- The before- and after-explosion events are both subscribed. If the before-event has no source, the after-event can still seed; a short cross-phase dedupe window prevents duplicate seeds. Added mocked checks for missing-source fallback and repeat-blast handling. This still requires a Bedrock test.
- Spore pods across all eight strains now tick every 20–40 game ticks (about 1–2 seconds) instead of 300–2,400 ticks, so each stage should become visibly active much sooner.
- The three hybrid plants now check growth every 40–80 ticks (about 2–4 seconds), down from 200–400 ticks (10–20 seconds).
- When two different hybrid species are both mature within about 3 blocks, they persistently cross-pollinate; each pollinated plant gives one extra harvest item, then clears the saved pollination state. This remains gated by the existing Hybrid Plants switch (off by default).
- Pollination discovery scans at most once per plant every 200 game ticks and keeps a 512-entry transient scan cache; the saved pollination state remains on the block.
- Expanded the saved, two-path Field Story from five to ten chapters. Garden and Ruin routes have different later objectives; progress and rewards still save before XP is granted.
- Water, Lava, and Phantom creeper movement switches are now reapplied when those entities load from an unloaded chunk, even if their one-time spawn setup already ran.
- Newly spread microscopic spores now appear after 1–5 seconds by default instead of waiting 5–60 seconds. Saved ranges changed away from the old defaults are preserved; untouched old defaults receive the faster range.
- Toxic-spore contact reactions now include all 18 listed animals/hostile mob types, with the existing villager warning and iron-golem colony response covering the other three named species.
- The 0.8.1 story foundation, all 23 creeper identifiers/artworks, carrier rules, and current systems are retained.

INSTALLATION
Import Creeper_Spores_0.8.31_Expanded.mcaddon with Minecraft closed, then open the imported pack and enable both behavior and resource packs on a disposable world. If Minecraft reports a duplicate, check whether the same version is already installed. Keep active worlds backed up before changing pack versions.
